💡 Budget-Friendly Community Secret

Build a ₹900 DIY Air Cleaner (Corsi-Rosenthal Box Fan)

Commercial air purifiers cost ₹10,000 to ₹35,000. You can build an equally powerful air filter using a simple box or table fan and a MERV-13 / True HEPA AC filter strapped tightly with masking tape.

₹950 DIY Filter Cost
85-90% PM2.5 Drop in 30 Mins
₹15,000+ Branded Machine Cost
How to Assemble at Home in 10 Minutes:
  1. Step 1: Get a MERV-13 or HEPA AC Filter: Available on Amazon or local AC parts market for ₹400-₹600. Look for dimensions matching your table fan or box fan (e.g. 16x16 or 12x12 inch).
  2. Step 2: Seal Against the INTAKE (Back): Place the filter firmly against the back intake grill of the fan (arrow pointing TOWARD the fan blades). Secure all 4 edges with brown packing tape or duct tape so zero air leaks around the sides.
  3. Step 3: Run on Medium in Bedroom: Run for 45 minutes in a closed bedroom before bedtime. It quietly scrubs airborne particles down to safe levels for kids and elders.
Why This Matters: Air purifiers are simply a fan pulling dirty air through a dense fibrous mesh. The DIY version moves the same volume of air through certified filter media at 1/15th the price.

Why Winter Smog Is Different: Quick Answers

Understanding the science makes protecting your family intuitive.

What is 'Thermal Inversion' and why is 6:00 AM the most dangerous time? ▾

In winter, the ground cools rapidly overnight. A layer of warm air sits above like a giant invisible lid (an 'inversion layer'), trapping vehicle exhaust, dust, and smoke right down at breathing height. Only when the midday sun (11:30 AM to 3:00 PM) warms the earth does this ceiling lift and pollutants disperse upward.

Why does a cloth handkerchief or surgical mask not protect against PM2.5? ▾

PM2.5 particles are microscopic—about 3% the width of a single human hair. A cotton handkerchief has gaps measuring 100 to 200 microns, allowing toxic particles to slide through unobstructed. Only certified N95, KN95, or FFP2 respirators with electrostatically charged meltblown fabric can trap over 95% of fine particles.

Why is dry brooming (jhadu) worse than wet mopping indoors? ▾

Dry brooming vigorously kicks settled dust and micro-soot back into the air, where it stays suspended at lung height for up to 4 hours. Wet mopping with a damp cloth traps dust particles on the floor surface and rinses them away safely.
